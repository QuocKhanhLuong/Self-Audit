## Common spaces and measures

For a finite cine pixel domain Ω with valid support, a prediction is p:Ω→Δ³, with names BG/RV/MYO/LV fixed before evaluation. UNKNOWN is a decision action outside that simplex. Evaluation uses one declared normalized measure ν (patient-balanced when comparing patient risk); training weights can induce a different measure. Logits ℓ∈R^(|Ω|×4) have an additive per-pixel gauge; fix class-centering when comparing their norms. Euclidean vector norms and L²(ν) function norms are distinguished. θ denotes network parameters; s denotes optimizer state. A prediction-space displacement is not automatically an attainable Δθ or an AdamW transition.

The permitted information I includes images X, valid acquisition metadata M, ontology, pretrained weights and lineage Θ, explicit semantic priors Π, and frozen TRAIN bank provenance. Features, anchors, Q and any justified Z are derived observations, not independent replications. Inference information V is declared separately from teacher/proposal information H. No dataset, weights or patient mask was downloaded or inspected in this session.

## T01 — Named identity and partial information

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE. **Statement:** named recovery is possible only when allowed observations and justified priors eliminate rival names; no task masks does not imply no semantic prior.

**Scope/assumptions:** a finite admissible world family W, fixed observations I and ontology; admissibility cannot be selected using hidden evaluation masks. Define S_i(I)={Y_i(w):w∈W,I(w)=I}. A predictor of I is correct at i in every compatible world iff |S_i(I)|=1. Necessity follows from identical inputs; sufficiency is the unique member, without a computability claim. If two compatible masks disagree on ν-mass d, any randomized learner has max(R₀,R₁)≥d/2: couple its randomness and add the two pointwise errors. This is a necessary information condition, not evidence that actual full cines always possess the constructed symmetry.

For a permitted candidate set S, loss −log Σ_(c∈S)p_c only constrains total set mass. S=all classes gives zero gradient; intra-set names remain interchangeable. Missing evidence, empty conflicting intersection, and repeated evidence are distinct states. Bright/blood-like appearance alone does not establish S={RV,LV}: an atrium, vessel or artifact can be BG under this ontology. A deterministic heuristic S=g(X) can have wrong-label inclusion probability one conditional on full X,Y, invalidating the ε<1 premise of the particular Cour consistency theorem; this is not an impossibility result for every restricted learner.

**Positive result:** for a fixed image region C_j, let its dominant true name c_j have impurity α_j. A weighted mixture t_j of named sources puts at least L_j=(1−β_j)(1−η_j) mass on c_j when bad-source weight≤β_j and every remaining source assigns≥1−η_j to c_j. Arbitrary dependence is allowed. If ||p_i−t_j||∞≤δ_j and 2L_j−1>2δ_j, then p_i uniquely predicts c_j: its margin is at least 2L_j−1−2δ_j. Error mass on selected complete regions is≤Σ_j α_jν(C_j). All impurity, true orientation and good-source-mass quantities are **latent**. A large observed vote margin does not certify them.

**Cross-review consequence:** this bound does not prove improvement over a teacher. A 90/10 mixed region with perfect pixel labels becomes 10% wrong after pooling, while all voting premises hold. A strict comparison needs teacher error on the same measure to exceed the impurity plus transfer budget. A sharper rare-class example has 99 BG pixels and 1 MYO pixel with a perfect pointwise teacher: pooling yields t=(.99,0,.01,0), aggregate error only .01 but MYO Dice falls1→0. An aggregate purity or margin guarantee is not a rare-class guarantee. Conversely, five homogeneous sites with four correct local hard anchors and one wrong one yield direct region-vote error 0 versus local-source error 1/5. No student is needed for that construction. R1's revised finite-measure lemma converts weighted KL D into additional disagreement≤2D/(w₀m₀²), plus low-weight and low-margin exception masses, using Pinsker and Markov. The margin is truth-oriented unless naming is separately established; it is not a no-GT certificate.

**Prior:** latent-class label permutation (Allman); partial labels (Cour); weak-source modeling (Ratner/Bach); region propagation. Mask-free echo work by Ferreira et al. supplies a positive empirical precedent with explicit view/clinical priors, not MRI or information-free naming. **Evidence:** R1-I1/I2/P1/S1/G1; R4 round1 comparator challenge; probes/R1. **Implication:** directly test region naming before student transfer. **Limits/prediction:** duplicate-source confidence and missing geometry cannot rescue indistinguishable worlds; a declared valid orienting prior must break the deliberately preserved permutation. Anatomical applicability remains unverified.

## T02 — The actual observation and the split are different objects

**Status:** CODE_FACT / DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE. **Statement:** resizing a teacher bank adds no independent observation; D/U preserves average logits, not average class probability or teacher truth.

**Scope/assumptions:** D averages complete equal cells of k=16 pixels; U repeats, DU=I; P=I−UD is the orthogonal projector in the uniform full-grid Euclidean metric. A separate linear teacher H_T is an analytic model only when justified; historical Q is not established as H_Tp*. Fixed interpolation R satisfies rank(RH_T)≤rank(H_T) and ker(H_T)⊆ker(RH_T). Reliability-weighted Q transport is conditional normalization of R(wQ) by Rw; it is not a new independent label measurement.

Every unbounded logit field has ℓ=Ua+Pr by a=Dℓ,r=ℓ. The actual bounded branch s=b tanh(r) restricts each class's detail range to<2b and |(Ps)_i|<2b(1−1/k). A fixed coarse class margin>4b(1−1/k) cannot flip anywhere under this branch; at b=1 this is 3.75. Pairwise log-odds variation within one cell is<4b even with freely trainable a. Thus binary .99/.01 confidence on opposing pixels, requiring 2log99≈9.19 variation, is impossible at b=1, though a less confident correct boundary may be possible. These are representation constraints, not claims that trainable coarse logits can never improve.

**Proof:** subtracting a mean preserves ranges; extremize one bounded entry against k−1 opposites; apply the bound twice for opposing classes. Finite tanh attains only strict interior, its closure admits equality. Softmax(mean logits)≠mean softmax(logits): logits4,0 give .880797 versus .741007. If H_T cells and D cells differ, neither kernel inclusion is automatic. Partial validity breaks valid-only mean conservation, and low-precision cancellation requires dtype/scale tolerances.

**Counterexamples:** shifted teacher cells observe a D-null perturbation; a partially padded cell is zero-mean over padded positions but not valid ones. **Prior:** null-space networks, regularizing networks, label proportions and feature upsampling. **Evidence:** R2-C1/C2/C4, R6-C1; probes/R2. **Implication:** hard split is a coordinate/ablation choice, not the assumed correct method. **Limits/prediction:** compare probability mass and argmax separately; re-encoding native shifted views can add feature observations, simple cache resizing cannot. No anatomical recovery follows from exact DP=0.

## T03 — A positive noisy recovery condition, and its obligations

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE. **Statement:** complementary image relations can resolve teacher-unobserved detail if their joint response has positive gain on the actual admissible differences.

**Scope/assumptions:** fixed finite-dimensional admissible set S, declared observation metric, A=[W_TH_T;√λK_X], y=[W_Tq;√λb_X]. Define α_S=inf_(u≠v∈S)||A(u−v)||/||u−v||>0. If p*∈S, ||Ap*−y||≤ε and a fitted p̂∈S has residual≤ε+ρ, then ||p̂−p*||≤(2ε+ρ)/α_S. Triangle inequality bounds the response difference and the definition of gain bounds its inverse. This allows correlated noise.

With p₀∈S, ||p₀−p*||≤η, ||A||≤L, and residual no worse than p₀ plus ρ, the bound becomes η+[2(ε+Lη)+ρ]/α_S. If A_true=A+Δ_A, ||Δ_A||≤τ and ||p*||≤B, add τB to observation error. These assumptions contain **latent anatomical consistency and approximation error**. Rescaling rows/λ can inflate α while also inflating error; compare the entire ratio in a fixed metric, never α alone.

**Constructive specialization:** K_X is an image-graph incidence matrix, p* constant on its components, and V contains normalized component indicators. The effective supported map W_TH_TV must have full column rank to identify region values, with LS error≤||weighted noise||/σ_min(W_TH_TV). Bare H_TV rank is insufficient: H_T=V=I₂ and W_T=diag(1,0) lose the second region. Two equally mixed regions observed only by their joint mean stay ambiguous; separate informative region measurements resolve the algebra. False bridges can make a graph appear better-conditioned while making its anatomy assumption false. For K_w=w[1,−1], every w>0 enforces equal values under a hard constraint although w→0; this changes S discontinuously and cannot be hidden as a small τ on a fixed S.

**Gauge/local extension:** on class-centered logits whose softmax probabilities along the whole connecting segment are≥m>0, m||Δℓ||≤||Δp||≤½||Δℓ||. For a nonlinear observation F with derivative gain σ and L-Lipschitz derivative, ||F(ℓ)−F(ℓ*)||≥(σ−LR/2)||ℓ−ℓ*|| inside the assumed radius R. A small residual does not establish that the estimated solution lies in that neighborhood. Saturation degrades m; a fixed softmax gauge does not remove semantic permutations.

**Hard/soft consequence:** exact H p̂=y with error e=y−Hp* forces ||p̂−p*||≥||e||/||H||. A residual ball contains truth only if its radius covers ||e||. Soft correction can outperform a biased exact fit or damage a correct teacher under a wrong image relation. No universal ordering or operational no-GT radius is proved.

**Prior:** Chandrasekaran restricted-gain stability; Schwab null-space/regularizing networks; GSNR graph-null representation. These are classical inverse-problem consequences, not new universal theorems. **Evidence:** R2-C5/C6/C7 and R3 round1 corrections; tiny graphs in probes/R2. **Implication:** specify one image relation and its failure model before architecture. **Limits/prediction:** repeat/noisy nearly dependent observations remain unstable; a falsely added edge may improve numerical uniqueness while worsening truth. Named semantics is a separate T01 obligation.

## T04 — Native appearance supplies a force, but only a conditional correction

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE / EMPIRICAL_OBSERVATION (manufactured). **Statement:** frozen uncertain-anchor intensity contrast can correct a binary blood–MYO interface; it cannot name RV versus LV or certify where it should act.

**Scope/assumptions:** complete mixed cells; y_i∈{0 MYO,1 blood}, x_i=μ_M+Δy_i+e_i, Δ>0, |e_i|≤σ. Anchor-estimated means differ from true means by≤δ and Δ>2δ. Costs c_B=(x−μ̂_B)²,c_M=(x−μ̂_M)² are frozen. Missing anchors disable the evidence. Let r=sigmoid(t₀+2s), t₀=log(expℓ_RV+expℓ_LV)−ℓ_MYO, fixed controlled logit direction v=(0,1,−1,1), Ps=s, |s|<B. Use E=Σ_jΣ_(i∈j) g_j[r_ic_Bi+(1−r_i)c_Mi] with cell-constant g_j>0.

**Formal native gradient:** at cell-constant r₀, P∇_sE=−4g r₀(1−r₀) Δ̂ Px. It is nonzero iff Δ̂≠0 and Px≠0 for finite nonsaturated logits and positive g. The anchor midpoint cancels. Its first step is therefore exactly a scaled centered-image contrast, a required cheap baseline. Pixel-varying weights or padding can introduce forces without new anatomical evidence.

**Noise-tolerant correction:** if true cell occupancy ρ_j∈(0,1) and m_j=Δ min(ρ_j,1−ρ_j)−2σ>0, then (2y_i−1)(Px)_i≥m_j. Write κ_j=4g_jr₀j(1−r₀j)Δ̂>0. For the step s⁺=ηκ_jPx_j, targeted wrong conditional margins M_j are all corrected if η>max_j M_j/(2κ_jm_j). The frozen energy has gradient Lipschitz constant bounded by L=||g(c_B−c_M)||∞. Energy descent and tanh feasibility also require η<min{2/L,min_j B/(κ_j||Px_j||∞)}. A **nonempty common interval** proves the three properties simultaneously; it need not exist. Very confident wrong logits, saturation or a thin minority can make it empty. Truth mixing, errors and noise constants remain latent.

**Proof:** center the measurement model, bound e_i−mean(e) by2σ; obtain signed image margin. A step shifts conditional odds by2ηκPx. The smooth descent lemma yields E(s⁺)≤E(0)−η(1−ηL/2)||P∇E||²; tanh inversion realizes feasible output s. It does not establish neural parameter attainability. Correcting binary errors without adding any makes Dice nondecrease for a nonempty reference class. Removing only FPs while TP=0 need not strictly improve Dice; empty structures need a declared convention.

**Network path:** at zero final detail head raw=Wh+b, ∇_WE=BΣ_i G_i h_iᵀ for G=P∇_ℓE. This can vanish even when G≠0 (constant within-cell h). Bias gradient and first-step upstream h gradient through the detail branch are zero under the stated complete-cell conditions. The separate coarse path and other losses can still update shared encoder/decoder parameters. The manufactured current-model fixture gives a nonzero head gradient/update, but its unrestricted energy gradient generally changes RV:LV odds; it does not realize the controlled v theorem. A realized conditional logit within e_transfer of a proposal preserves its decisions when e_transfer<min|t_proposal|; preserving *correct* decisions requires a latent true signed margin. BG probabilities and even the winning name can change inside edited support on the controlled v path: logits(.2,−2,0,.1)+.2v change BG to LV. Conditional blood–MYO odds with |t₀|≥2B cannot flip under |s|<B. These are computable output obstructions, not evidence that a feasible edit is anatomically right.

**Counterexamples/evidence:** probes/R3 has binary errors8→0 under ordered anchors,8→16 under swapped anchors with identical energy decrease; pure blood errors0→8. Non-heart rings, absent MYO/RV, contrast inversion and source bias remain real obligations, not solved by gating on confidence. **Prior:** classical appearance energies, weakly supervised regularized loss; T08 direct-step equivalence. **Implication:** at most a transparent conditional native-interface baseline, not a full named segmentation architecture. **Limits/prediction:** centered contrast should match its initial direction; if it does, no extra mechanism contribution is established. Incorrect or absent evidence must not be silently relabeled as valid supervision.

## T05 — Distillation can remove nuisance or discard truth

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE. **Statement:** capacity alone does not distinguish these two outcomes.

**Scope/assumptions:** V⊆H, T(H)∈Δ³, nonnegative integrable W, and unrestricted measurable p(V). Weighted CE is minimized by m_W=E[WT|V]/E[W|V] where the denominator is positive. Excess objective equals E[E[W|V] KL(m_W||p)]. At zero support the semantic objective supplies no condition. Batch self-normalization must be included in W; this is not an optimizer convergence theorem.

On a common unweighted or explicitly reweighted evaluation law, let m=E[T|V], η_H=E[Y|H], η_V=E[Y|V], s²=E||T−m||², c=E〈T−m,η_H−η_V〉. Then R(T)−R(m)=s²−2c for Brier risk R. Expand the squared difference and use E[T−m|V]=0. If ||p−m||₂≤δ and b=||m−η_V||₂, then R(p)−R(T)≤−s²+2c+2bδ+δ². Removed variation helps only when the covariance and transfer cost are small enough; truth-dependent quantities are latent.

**Critical applicability correction:** if Q is a deterministic function of exactly the student's full V, m=T and s=c=0. A larger unrestricted student has no conditional-averaging denoising advantage. Finite-sample regularization, restricted information or a new image constraint are distinct mechanisms requiring their own evidence.

**Counterexample:** teacher values.5/.9 with constant V produce average.7. When the variation is nuisance, teacher Brier.50→student.42. With identical observed V,T but hidden true probabilities.5/.9, teacher.34→student.42. No certificate from V,T alone can label both correctly. Reliability weights can bias an otherwise unbiased target. Q=(1−h)u+hZ, when actually justified, preserves class ordering for h>0; inversion amplifies errors like1/h and Q alone does not identify h. Z-weighted and hZ-weighted training are different policies.

**Prior:** Noise2Noise, generalized distillation, classical proper-loss projection. **Evidence:** R4-C1/C2, R1-I3, probes/R4 and R1. **Implication/prediction:** separately control input context, initialization and target policy; verify same-observable worlds reverse truth ordering. **Limits:** no Dice or patient-generalization claim; current full-Q policy remains unchanged.

## T06 — Feedback and transfer have explicit, latent quality obligations

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT / CONJECTURE (applicability). **Statement:** some biased noisy proposals improve predictions, but recurrence algebra does not show the actual proposal is one of them.

**Scope/assumptions:** Hilbert-valued prediction functions on a fixed fresh evaluation distribution independent of training history F_t; every proposal is representable from declared V for the displayed transfer formulation. Let μ_t=E[A_t|F_t], ||μ_t−η||₂≤ρ||p_t−η||₂+β with ρ<1, conditional centered proposal variance≤σ². Relax B_t=(1−a)p_t+aA_t,0<a≤1, and assume RMS student transfer error≤δ_t. Then conditional RMS next error≤sqrt(((1−a+aρ)e_t+aβ)²+a²σ²)+δ_t. Centered cross terms vanish; Minkowski supplies transfer slack. Independence of successive rounds is not needed beyond the stated conditional moments.

A conservative iterated bound is E_t≤k^tE₀+[a(β+σ)+δ](1−k^t)/(1−k), k=1−a+aρ. This admits nonzero bias/noise, but ρ,β,σ and η are latent. A_t=p_t hasρ=1 and no contraction; incorrect common bias survives EMA. Weight EMA is not output averaging. Useful self-distillation can change regularization without adding data; it still needs a specified mechanism.

For one frozen V-measurable proposal A and actual student p, let d=||p−A||₂. Then R(p)≤R(A)+2||A−η||₂d+d². A true proposal advantage g survives if this slack<g. Since simplex distance≤√2,2√2d+d² is a conservative known-form slack, but the true gap g is still unknown. Proposal quality and transfer must be reported separately; low d can perfectly copy a semantic swap. Weighted support agreement is not dense agreement: with W=(1,0), a student can match a two-pixel proposal exactly on the first row yet have dense RMS discrepancy .8. Empty support is NA. If identical V hides equiprobable proposals(.1,.9) and(.9,.1), every same-V predictor has transfer MSE at least .32; whether averaging helps truth is the separate T05 question.

**Prior:** Wei self-training theorem requires latent worst-class pseudo error<1/3 plus expansion/separation; Mobahi regularization; MeanTeacher uses supervised signal. **Evidence:** R4-C4/C5/C6, R6-C3/C5, fixed finite constructions. **Implication:** freeze a proposal and compare direct energy before introducing feedback. **Limits/prediction:** corruption and transfer sweeps cross explicit boundaries in toys; no-GT consistency cannot certify real contraction or true-RV protection.

## T07 — Protect actual updates and distinguish proxy groups from anatomy

**Status:** CODE_FACT / DERIVED_RESULT / COUNTEREXAMPLE. **Statement:** average KL, raw-gradient alignment and fixed pseudo-stratum retention are different claims from true rare-class safety.

**Scope/assumptions:** a fixed proxy-defined measure ν_j; true CE risk R_j has L_j*-Lipschitz gradient along the entire actual displacement Δ; ||∇R_j−ĝ_j||≤δ_j. Then ΔR_j≤ĝ_jᵀΔ+δ_j||Δ||+(L_j*/2)||Δ||² by the descent lemma and Cauchy–Schwarz. For Δ=−ηd, positive m_j=ĝ_jᵀd−δ_j||d|| allows 0<η≤2m_j/(L_j*||d||²). Constants are latent; same-measure target noise gives δ_j≤E_νj||J_i||op||r_i−q_i||. This excludes actual RV omitted from the pseudo-RV group and does not address wrong geometry.

**Counterexamples:** shared-logit total KL.595108→.244823 while rare-stratum KL.595108→1.195223. Independent logits improve the rare class slowly, whereas equal weighting on collided representations stalls: imbalance, conflict and capacity are distinct. Raw direction d=(−1,3), guard gradient(2,1), dot=1 is locally protective for additive descent; first Adam scaling gives Δ≈(.1,−.1), guard loss.5→.605. Full optimizer rollback differs from parameter-only rollback. Strict nonincrease at wrong proxy optimum L_Q=θ²/2 prevents every nonzero correction toward truth risk(θ−1)²/2.

**Function implication:** any guard must evaluate the full changed parameter path or actually staged optimizer transition, including state/buffers. An anchor-relative proxy cap and cumulative positive-variation debt are different policies; neither is an anatomical tolerance. A repeatedly used acceptance panel is optimizer-facing TRAIN data, not an independent diagnostic panel. Feature cosine alone leaves norms and head drift uncontrolled: for z=Wf+b, ||Δz||≤||W||||Δf||+||ΔW||||f||+||ΔW||||Δf||+||Δb||.

**Prior:** GEM/PCGrad/CAGrad, GradNorm/GroupDRO, LP-FT. **Evidence:** R5-C01–C07, H01/H02 revisions; probes/R5. **Implication:** compare fixed normalization, smaller encoder LR and frozen/warm-up controls before a new guard. **Limits/prediction:** at matched actual displacement and compute, any guard benefit must exceed step-size/stall effects; wrong-source locks must remain visible. No true-RV safety certificate follows from proxy retention.

## T08 — A detached proposal can be exactly the direct-energy update

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT; discovered during R5↔R6 Round1 and corrected for weighted metrics. **Statement:** adding a proposal stage is not automatically adding a different learning mechanism.

**Scope/assumptions:** b_θ is differentiable and strictly interior to a finite product of four-class simplexes, b₀=b_θ₀, J its parameter Jacobian. P is block-diagonal with blocks I₄−11ᵀ/4, hence PJ=J. For differentiable frozen E, choose τ>0 small enough that detached t=b₀−τP∇E(b₀) remains in the simplex. Let L(θ)=||b_θ−t||²/(2τ). Then ∇_θL|θ₀=JᵀP∇E(b₀)=Jᵀ∇E(b₀), exactly the direct-energy gradient. Chain rule and 1ᵀJ_i=0 prove it. For positive pixel weights M=diag(ν_i)⊗I₄, M commutes with P; using t=b₀−τPM⁻¹∇E and L=||b_θ−t||²_M/(2τ) preserves the identity. Zero-weight entries are omitted. An analogous scalar spatial projection needs its own PJ=J condition; do not substitute the student's D/U projector silently. Finite projection/clipping, multiple steps, remote or refreshed origins, CE rather than squared matching, different coordinates and optimizer histories can break the identity and need explicit controls.

**Counterexample to a novelty claim:** one detached tensor step followed by squared distillation at the same point may be only a rescaled loss. A comparison against an unmatched larger learning rate is confounded. **Closest prior:** direct regularized losses and proposal/alternating optimization in Tang et al.; elementary chain-rule identity, not novel general theorem. **Evidence:** R5-R1-D1 proof; R6-C3 revised; actual review records; coordinator five-seed float64 gradient check has maximum discrepancy 2.93e−15, with wrong-metric and stale-origin counterexamples in probes/coordinator/proposal_gradient_equivalence.json. Equal complete gradients plus equal deterministic optimizer state also give the same single AdamW update; there is no categorical AdamW exception to that exact identity. **Implication:** match origin, step count, refresh, metric, gradient scale, actual parameter displacement and compute before attributing gains to proposals. **Limits/prediction:** exact manufactured one-step gradients must match under the premises; a difference then indicates an implementation/coordinate defect. No anatomy claim follows either way.

## T09 — Convex probability upsampling cannot undo donor class domination

**Status:** DERIVED_RESULT, classical convexity; no novelty claim. **Statement:** useful edge interpolation can still lack the ability to promote a consistently dominated class. **Scope/assumptions:** p_ic=Σ_j K_ij q_jc, K_ij≥0, Σ_jK_ij=1, identical weights for all channels, declared donor support. Then min_j q_jc≤p_ic≤max_j q_jc. If q_j,RV≤q_j,BG for every positive-weight donor, p_i,RV≤p_i,BG. Multiply each inequality by K_ij and sum. Exactly absent mass remains absent; positive Q floors do not defeat pairwise domination. Composed row-stochastic mixing has the same property. Strict domination holds if any strictly dominated donor has positive weight; ties require a stated rule, not recovered semantic evidence.

**Counterexample:** every donor favors BG over RV, even with nonzero RV mass; a class-shared convex image-guided kernel cannot make RV strictly beat BG. **Closest prior:** direct probability-map guided/JBU upsampling, including the scoped Upsample Anything control. **Evidence:** R6-C3-L1; R3 Round2 setting challenge; R2 supplementary mathematical review. **Implication:** test class-support/ordering before adding an upsampler. **Limits:** not a theorem for arbitrary transformed features, negative or class-dependent weights, semantic heads or subsequent student optimization. No donor-domination frequency was measured on patients. **Falsifiable prediction:** the manufactured dominance case is invariant under any kernel satisfying these assumptions; violation indicates an undeclared operator. Named correctness of a mechanism escaping the hull remains unproved.

## Gap taxonomy

- **Execution/recipe limitation:** active detail head has no enabled native objective. A functioning DINO architecture is not evidence of trained native refinement. This is not itself a novel scientific gap.
- **Known family limitation:** dependent weak sources, label permutation, teacher uncertainty, refinement ambiguity and biased self-training have established literature. Larger networks or renamed modules do not resolve them automatically.
- **Missing mathematical specification:** actual Q-to-native observation relation, truth-relevant image relation, valid parameter-to-output transfer, fixed risk measure and target adaptation protocol are not supplied by the old split algebra.
- **Potential task-level research gap:** obtain useful native corrections from a declared single image measurement despite imperfect named anchors, and preserve their benefit in a fixed-input student. The remaining obligations are image/anatomy alignment, semantic orientation, conditional error tolerance and transfer/generalization; none is closed by the toy results.
