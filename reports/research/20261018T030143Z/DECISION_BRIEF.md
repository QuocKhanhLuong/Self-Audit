## Decision in plain language

The useful research direction is **evidence-qualified native correction and its transfer to a fixed-input student**. The central unknown is which permitted observation corrects a coarse teacher error, and under which failures that correction survives. Increasing the backbone, forcing every pixel into a name, or adding feedback does not answer that question.

There is a constructive opportunity: an image relation can resolve some teacher-invisible directions, and a frozen appearance contrast can correct some mixed blood–MYO cells despite noisy anchors. There are equally concrete failures: the same energy decrease can make all binary labels wrong; pure cells can acquire false tissue; region pooling can erase a correctly detected minority structure. These findings warrant a narrow mechanism comparison, not approval of a final architecture.

The smallest approval-worthy next object is H06's fixed native-appearance/direct-loss study with its exact centered-intensity comparator. H11 student transfer follows only if a proposal advantage survives the stated controls. Neither is claimed novel or anatomically successful. A simpler established method may close the gap; if so, use and credit it rather than add a module.

## Complete architecture-independent problem

**Inputs and permissions.** Training may use declared TRAIN cines X, validity-aware acquisition metadata M, fixed ontology O={BG,RV,MYO,LV}, reviewed pretrained feature weights Θ, explicit anatomical priors Π, and uncertain named anchors/bank Q with support and lineage. No hidden patient masks may create regions, select gates, calibrate trust radii, choose checkpoints or tune hyperparameters. A hypothetical conditional Z requires a separately justified source decomposition h; current full-Q policy is unchanged. Semantic permissions may be partial: an image edge does not name a chamber, and blood-like appearance does not exclude BG.

**Target.** Learn pθ(X) at native resolution with fixed named channels and an optional abstention action G. UNKNOWN=255 is outside O; it is not a learned fifth class and is not BG. Anonymous partition quality, partial exclusion knowledge, named segmentation and external semantic generalization are separate endpoints.

**Inference contract.** The initial proposed comparison uses the same image input available to the current student and frozen parameters. Training-time cine metadata/anchors may construct a proposal only under declared permissions. If full temporal context is required at inference, it is a separate matched-input design. A fixed per-cine preprocessing function is distinct from per-image parameter fitting and from fitting statistics/prototypes on the target cohort. Any such adaptation is reported explicitly, never included silently in a frozen inference claim.

**Desired objective.** Improve latent named patient-balanced risk R(pθ) and native structure while retaining useful source evidence; operational weighted CE, feature cosine and image energy are surrogate objectives, not R. A candidate may solve min L_bank(pθ)+λE_X(pθ) under a declared output/edit budget, but λ, source support and edit coordinates must be specified before evaluation. Missing evidence disables that additional term and remains missing; conflict does not become a forced label. Training objective changes cannot be bundled with target-policy/sampling/backbone changes and attributed to one mechanism.

**Established obstruction.** Identical allowed observations with rival named masks preclude universal correctness. Even with names fixed, teacher-null detail remains unresolved unless an image relation distinguishes it on the admissible set. Learned or hard constraints can choose a unique wrong solution. No-GT diagnostics cannot universally distinguish coherent wrong anchors from valid ones.

**Assumptions for progress.** An applicable symmetry-breaking semantic source; an image relation with limited anatomical misspecification; enough supported independent measurement directions; nondegenerate contrast or restricted gain; attainable/learnable output edits; and transfer/generalization error smaller than any proposal advantage. The actual anatomical constants are latent. Their mathematical role is explicit; their numerical value is not inferred from confidence.

**Evaluation measure.** Freeze predictions and the protocol before any separately authorized reference scoring. Report fixed-name dense Dice separately for RV/MYO/LV, patient macro mean and distribution/tail, boundary error with physical spacing and explicit empty-case handling, false-positive fraction/physical volume on absent classes, and BG separately. Compute class Dice over reference-present patients with their counts and a predeclared aggregation; report reference-absent patients separately rather than inserting empty-empty Dice=1 into the same macro. Dense raw predictions are scored on the full valid domain. Selective output is additional: report coverage, rejection/error mass and retained risk by true class and patient; rejected true-class pixels remain false negatives for selective Dice, never vanish from denominators or turn into BG. Risk at zero coverage is undefined. Provide full confusion counts including abstention; calibration/Brier and confidence are distinct from segmentation. Any development-exposed cohort stays development-exposed. External claims distinguish frozen inference, per-cine fitting and cohort adaptation. Paired patient-level uncertainty is preferable to treating pixels as independent replicates.

## At most two central research questions

### RQ1 / H06 — When does one native image observation add a useful correction?

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** under imperfect but correctly oriented anchor contrast and a genuine mixed-interface measurement regime, frozen native appearance can improve a coarse proposal on a nontrivial region of a prespecified corruption sweep; its added complexity is justified only if it exceeds an exactly matched simple image contrast or established direct-energy baseline. This is a conditional research target, not a universal named RV/MYO/LV claim.

**Minimal sketch.** Freeze μ̂_B,μ̂_M from permitted named evidence. Form c_B=(X−μ̂_B)²,c_M=(X−μ̂_M)² and E_X=Σg[r c_B+(1−r)c_M], where r is blood probability conditional on blood/MYO. Under the controlled scalar edit s in direction v=(0,1,−1,1), its first output step is s⁺=k_j P X_j, k_j=4ηg_jr₀j(1−r₀j)(μ̂_B−μ̂_M). That exact cell-scaled contrast is the cheapest comparator, not an optional weak baseline.

**Insertion point if later approved.** Output-level research adapter immediately after LayerGuidedStudent.forward in `layer_upgrade/model.py:102–114`, before a new native loss at `training_v1/runner.py:433–470`; existing `prepare_targets` remains the historical Q transport. First compare a direct tensor proposal and controlled scalar edit, then distinguish the existing unrestricted detail head. No production insertion has been made.

**New information and gradient.** Fine native intensities supply within-cell observations absent from a resized bank. They supply a contrast, not RV/LV identity. P∇E can be nonzero but must also correlate with detail features to create ∇W_detail. The first zero-head update can reach final weights while upstream detail features receive zero gradient through that branch; shared parameters may still change through the coarse path or other losses. BG probability and winning class can change even inside the controlled support; uncontrolled edits may also change RV:LV odds. The example logits(.2,−2,0,.1)+.2v changes BG to LV. The observable condition |t₀|≥2B rules out a conditional-odds flip within this edit bound; passing that check does not certify correct anatomy. When anchors are absent/equal or native image is constant, the corresponding added force is zero/disabled. No gate currently certifies true mixing; an oracle mixed-cell mask is permitted only as a manufactured upper bound, never an operational selection rule.

**Kill criterion.** Abandon a new-method claim if exact centered contrast/direct energy matches it; abandon anatomical efficacy claims if improvements need oracle cell selection, disappear under matched confidence, erase rare/pure structures, or reverse under permitted anchor/contrast perturbations without a defensible restricted scope. An intentionally indistinguishable coherent-corruption case may remain undetectably wrong; demanding universal detection would itself be invalid.

### RQ2 / H11 — Can a fixed student preserve an independently qualified proposal gain?

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** conditional on a fixed proposal advantage on a declared information/support measure, transfer into the same student/initialization can preserve enough margin to beat the same coarse baseline without merely trading RV errors for BG improvement. The comparison first tests direct energy versus detached one-step matching; feedback is excluded initially.

**Minimal sketch.** Freeze proposal A(X,Q) and its lineage. Compare L_direct=L_bank+λE_X(pθ) with L_transfer=L_bank+λ||pθ−sg(A)||²_ν/(2τ), using common coordinates and supported weights. For an A made by one small same-origin metric-gradient step, the gradients can be exactly equal; this equality is a mandatory positive test. For a genuinely distinct fixed A, measure proposal quality separately from d=||pθ−A||₂ on the declared common domain. Supported weighted discrepancy can be0 while full-domain discrepancy is.8 in a two-pixel example; unavailable proposal entries remain undefined, never fabricated. A true gap g survives only if 2||A−η||₂d+d²<g; g and η remain latent on patients until authorized evaluation.

**Insertion and gradient.** Same loss assembly point, existing encoder/decoder/heads, with exact declared head/encoder freeze schedule. A detached target prevents reconstruction/target collapse but does not make it correct. Existing feature and coarse losses continue as specified in a future versioned contract. If proposal support is absent, no transfer loss is manufactured there. Compare frozen encoder, full tuning, decoder warm-up and smaller encoder LR as standard controls, not new central mechanisms. A guard is deferred until simpler allocation/displacement controls fail.

**Kill criterion.** If the proposal itself has no advantage, stop transfer claims. If it improves but student fails at matched information, inspect reachability/capacity/optimization separately. If direct energy or lower LR matches the result, reject added proposal/guard complexity. Proxy-RV retention does not establish real-RV safety, particularly when the source has omitted true RV.

## Sequential experiment decision tree

All later execution requires explicit approval; these are specifications, not queued jobs.

1. **Information and source prerequisites.** Fix permitted semantic rules, bank lineage, geometry/missingness, ontology and inference input. No per-class semantic source → partial semantics/abstention; no invented membership. A semantic-first alternative is direct image-region naming (H07) with the exact same sources, before any student. It is a meaningful change of problem emphasis, not a third recommended architecture.
2. **Cheapest manufactured discriminator.** Same stored observation with reversed native arrangements, wrong/coherent anchors, duplicate sources, pure/empty/thin regions, shifted grids, constant/shuffled images, missing orientation. Check exact cell-scaled contrast and direct-energy/proposal identity. Known toy truth scores mechanisms; it does not select a patient gate. Failure of an asserted algebraic contract is a software defect; failure only outside stated premises narrows applicability.
3. **One evidence mechanism.** Sweep anchor bias/dependence, contrast/noise, minority occupancy and source confidence separately. Compare no detail, fixed JBU/region baseline, frozen appearance step/direct energy. Keep Q/support/sampling/architecture/init fixed. Report attainable support and collateral BG/RV/LV changes, not energy alone. If no useful regime survives without oracle selection, do not escalate model capacity.
4. **Proposal versus transfer.** Freeze proposals; record proposal-native quality separately from student approximation. Match exact initial gradients where appropriate. Use the same architecture/init first; capacity and pretraining are independent later factors. A fresh teacher-view method must match teacher-call and feature-exposure budgets against aligned averaging/native-crop baselines. A chronology-specific claim also needs an order-free pooling baseline with the same frames: surviving a time shuffle is not itself leakage when an unordered frame bag legitimately supplies information. No weights are obtained in Phase I.
5. **Optimization only after evidence survives.** Measure fixed normalized proxy strata, inactive groups, gradients and actual parameter/output displacement. Compare lower LR, fixed encoder, warm-up and the same-AdamW-displacement backtracking before a custom guard. Use separate comparisons for matched exposure, matched compute and descriptive displacement; these cannot generally all be matched simultaneously. Track attempted/rejected/accepted updates and stalls. Do not define a patient regression budget from unavailable target masks.
6. **Separately approved anatomical evaluation.** Freeze candidate/control predictions and selection rules; score full classwise and patient denominators once on the authorized split. No tuning on that result without declaring development exposure. A proposal-only gain, transfer-only fit, confidence change and native named improvement are different outcomes.
7. **External inference claim.** Only after useful in-domain evidence, test declared fixed preprocessing and frozen parameters; any per-cine optimization or cohort-fit variant gets a separate protocol. Failure under missing geometry or shifted appearance narrows the domain claim, not a reason to silently add target adaptation.
