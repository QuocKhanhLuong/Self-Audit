**Initial finding.** Named cardiac segmentation is neither automatically identifiable from unlabeled images nor universally impossible without task annotations. It becomes possible only to the extent that the allowed observation model and explicit semantic priors rule out rival named interpretations. A stronger encoder can reveal a useful partition, and a region-wise denoiser can correct a minority of wrong anchors, but neither supplies a missing name-to-anatomy relation. The strongest positive result below tolerates arbitrary dependence of anchor errors, at the cost of explicit LATENT contamination and region-purity assumptions. It already has a non-neural region-voting baseline.

## 1. Information ledger and prediction contract

Let a full cine be (X=(X_{t,z,u,v})\), with its finite valid-pixel domain \(\Omega_X\), physical sampling and time ordering. Let \(Y:\Omega_X\to\mathcal O\) denote the unobserved task segmentation, where \(\mathcal O=\{\mathrm{BG},\mathrm{RV},\mathrm{MYO},\mathrm{LV}\}\). The ontology includes label definitions and annotation conventions, not just four strings: which myocardium, basal/apical inclusion, papillary/trabeculation handling, and what BG means. Equal physical anatomy need not imply equal masks under different conventions. UNKNOWN=255 is an abstention action outside \(\mathcal O\).

Let \(\Theta\) be reviewed pretrained weights and upstream lineage, \(\Pi\) explicit image/anatomical priors, and \(B\) the hash-pinned TRAIN bank. Use \(I_{\rm train}=(X_{1:n},M_{1:n},\Theta,\Pi,\mathcal O,B)\). All derived arrays are recorded, but redundant arrays do not count as independent information.

| Item | What is observed / information source | What it can justify | What it does not justify without additional assumptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw \(X\) | Full cine intensities and observed sampling | Appearance, motion, image edges, candidate regions | A universal named interpretation or a unique annotation convention |
| Metadata \(M=(m,v,r)\) | Values, validity flags \(v\), missingness \(r\), conversion provenance | Physical orientation/spacing/time when valid; coordinate-consistent priors | Chamber identity solely from a cardinal coordinate; a missing affine is not evidence for default anatomy |
| Ontology \(\mathcal O\) | Human-specified class meanings/conventions | Defines the requested answer and allowed class relations | Which image region has which name |
| Priors \(\Pi\) | Explicit reviewed class/shape/topology/view relations | May break semantic symmetries if true and applicable | Universal validity under view changes, pathology, truncation, or missing geometry |
| Features \(F=f_\Theta(X)\) | Deterministic transformed image plus learned upstream prior \(\Theta\) | Useful representation, correspondence and partition proposals | A calibrated named RV/MYO/LV classifier merely from feature agreement |
| Anchors \(A_s\) | Named scores or justified set constraints, support, source ID, lineage, prerequisites | Location where naming assumptions enter; uncertain local semantic evidence | Correctness because several descendants agree; set membership from visual similarity alone |
| Historical \(Q\) | Frozen full four-channel distribution with lineage | A training target; output of historical assumptions and propagation | Ground-truth posterior or independent validation of those assumptions |
| \(h\) | Source-decomposition support, only if defined and recoverable | Amount of evidence in a specific mixture model | Correctness; uniqueness from \(Q\) alone; evidence for unsupported classes |
| \(Z\) | Conditional evidence derived from a valid \((Q,h)\) decomposition | Conditional target where \(h>0\), with a conditioning cost | Added observations, recovered semantics, or valid values at \(h=0\) |
| Reliability / permissions | Policy quantities derived from bank/prior rules | Eligibility, abstention and loss weighting under that policy | Calibrated risk, anatomical truth, or proof a never-supported class is learned |
| Student state \(\hat\theta\) | Function of permitted TRAIN information and optimizer randomness | A fixed deployment function | New independent supervision or target-domain risk certification |

The fixed single-cine inference contract is
\[
 (\hat Y,G)=H_{\hat\theta}(X_*,M_*^{\rm permitted}),\qquad
 \hat\theta=\mathcal A(I_{\rm train},U),
\]
where \(U\) is algorithmic randomness and \(G\) is an acceptance mask. If the required contract is strictly image-only, set the metadata argument to absent and explain how TRAIN metadata-dependent supervision transfers. Per-cine normalization may use the whole current cine; fitting normalization, prototypes, thresholds or weights on a target cohort is a separate transductive/adaptation setting. Feeding teacher banks, adjacent patients, manual view labels, semantic source heads or inferred target-cohort labels at inference changes the contract.

**Four distinct goals.** (i) anonymous partition recovery, evaluated up to an allowed permutation; (ii) partial semantic knowledge, e.g. a justified excluded class or candidate set; (iii) named RV/MYO/LV recovery on the observed cine; (iv) named generalization to a new patient/domain under a fixed inference function. Passing one does not establish the next. Even BG versus cardiac tissue needs an applicable definition and exclusion argument; blood-like regions outside target cavities can be BG under this ontology.

## 2. Evidence card R1-I1 — necessary information condition and residual symmetries

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT and COUNTEREXAMPLE; classical identifiability reasoning, not a novelty claim.
- **Plain statement:** if two allowed worlds look identical to the learner but require different named labels, no algorithm can guarantee the right name in both.
- **Scope:** any learner using the allowed observations; information limit, not an optimizer or architecture claim.
- **Assumptions:** fix a family \(\mathcal W\) of worlds consistent with acquisition, ontology and *explicit* semantic priors. A world specifies observed information \(I\), latent masks and their law. Its admissibility must not be defined using hidden evaluation masks.
- **Formal claim:** in the deterministic finite case, define the identification set
  \[
  \mathcal S_i(I)=\{Y_i(w):w\in\mathcal W,\ I(w)=I\}.
  \]
  A function of \(I\) can be correct in every compatible world at pixel \(i\) iff \(|\mathcal S_i(I)|=1\). Necessity follows because the same input must produce the same answer; sufficiency is the unique element. This is an existence statement, not a computability or estimation guarantee.
- **Statistical form:** for a specified true joint law, the best achievable pixel 0–1 risk from \(I\) is \(\mathbb E[1-\max_c P(Y_i=c\mid I)]\); exact almost-sure recovery requires a point-mass conditional distribution. This posterior is LATENT. Nonzero mutual information alone is insufficient for exact recovery.
- **Minimal counterexample / proof:** two anonymous regions of equal weight have identical allowed observations in worlds \(w_0,w_1\); labels are \((\mathrm{RV},\mathrm{LV})\) and \((\mathrm{LV},\mathrm{RV})\). All anchors are absent or symmetric, \(Q\) is uniform, and permitted priors do not orient the chambers. For any randomized prediction \(\hat Y\), at each differing pixel the sum of its two error indicators is at least one. Thus \(R_0+R_1\ge1\) and \(\max(R_0,R_1)\ge1/2\). More generally if the two deterministic masks disagree on normalized mass \(d\), \(\max R\ge d/2\). The common law of observations lets us couple the learner randomness. Our two-pixel exhaustive arithmetic probe attains the 1/2 minimax value.
- **Residual group:** a label permutation \(\pi\) belongs to the residual symmetry group only if applying it to latent interpretations preserves the allowed observed law **and every justified prior**. An ontology list alone leaves all name assignments open; a valid BG exclusion or myocardium relation can remove some permutations; an actual applicable oriented chamber relation can remove RV/LV swapping. Missing/invalid geometry expands the admissible family and may restore symmetries. Geometry is not the only possible asymmetry: image morphology plus a valid semantic model may suffice without orientation metadata.
- **Counterexample to an overbroad negative claim:** if \(\Pi\) correctly specifies a deterministic, observable relation that uniquely maps every candidate region to \(\mathcal O\), no target masks are logically necessary. Therefore “no task masks implies named segmentation is impossible” is rejected. The task-level open question is whether the available priors are accurate and rich enough, especially for RV and ontology conventions.
- **Closest prior:** latent-class label swapping in Allman–Matias–Rhodes, §2 and Corollary 2; indistinguishable-target construction in Ben-David et al., §5, Theorems 1–2. These concern different generative/DA settings; our elementary restriction is written specifically for the permitted information ledger.
- **Code/evidence:** common contract; probe `probes/R1/information_probes.py`, result `two_world_minimax_hamming=0.5`. No patient instance claims.
- **Implication:** locate exactly which source/rule removes each rival named assignment before optimizing a larger network. A valid singleton can be certified only relative to an adequately specified world family.
- **Limitations:** a manufactured symmetry does not establish that *real full cines* remain symmetric under all plausible permitted anatomy priors. Conversely a plausible prior does not establish its truth.
- **Falsifying control:** on a synthetic case whose priors intentionally preserve RV/LV swapping, permute named outputs consistently and verify every unlabeled quality score is unchanged. If a proposed no-GT semantic certificate distinguishes these worlds, its proof has an undeclared source or a false premise.

## 3. Evidence card R1-I2 — dependence, upstream lineage and apparent corroboration

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT; source-model claims below are LITERATURE_RESULT.
- **Plain statement:** several transformations of the same evidence can be useful, but their agreement is not several independent confirmations of a chamber name.
- **Scope/assumptions:** \(F,A,Q,Z\) are functions of \((X,M,\Theta,\Pi,B)\) and independent algorithmic seeds; pretrained weights and clinical priors are included explicitly in the conditioning information.
- **Formal claim/proof:** when \(D=d(I,U)\) with \(U\perp Y\mid I\), \(Y\to I\to D\) is a Markov chain, so \(I(Y;D\mid I)=0\). Adding \(D\) cannot distinguish the observationally identical worlds of R1-I1. This does **not** say pretrained weights carry no prior knowledge, or that transformed features offer no computational/sample-efficiency benefit. Omitting \(\Theta\) from \(I\) would make that overstatement invalid.
- **Dependent counterexample:** one wrong heuristic is duplicated 100 times; four good sources are each included once. Naive equal voting picks the wrong class with score 0.9615 in the manufactured probe. Assigning fixed total weight per source lineage retains the original correct aggregate. No independent-error concentration theorem applies to repeated copies.
- **Closest positive literature:** Allman et al.'s Corollary 2 identifies a three-view latent model only up to label swapping when all mixing weights are positive and the three conditional-probability matrices have Kruskal ranks summing to at least \(2r+2\). Ratner et al.'s 2016 theorem uses an accurately specified source model, semantic accuracy restrictions and additional sufficiency/generalization assumptions. Neither proves arbitrary image-derived correlated anchors identify anatomy.
- **Missing step:** identify source dependence and establish semantic orientation/accuracy under the actual image regime. Different code paths or different augmentations do not establish conditional independence given \(Y\). Independence is possible for appropriate views but must be justified, not assumed false universally either.
- **Implication / limitation:** lineage grouping prevents one simple duplication artifact but cannot repair a common false anatomical prior shared by all groups. “Independent worker agreement” also is not empirical source independence.
- **Falsifying control:** replicate an anchor descendant, randomize only redundant augmentations, and drop an entire upstream lineage. Predictions/confidence should not become arbitrarily certain merely by duplication. The unchanged-prediction outcome is only a dependence sanity check.

## 4. Evidence card R1-I3 — what Q-to-Z can and cannot identify

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT and COUNTEREXAMPLE; alternative target policy remains PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED.
- **Plain statement:** conditioning can remove an explicitly modeled uninformative mixture, but support must have provenance; inversion does not manufacture named information and can amplify error.
- **Scope:** the particular mixture \(Q=(1-h)u+hZ\), \(u=(1/K,\ldots,1/K)\), \(K=4\); not a claim that every bank satisfies it.
- **Assumptions:** \(Q,Z\in\Delta^{K-1}\), \(0<h\le1\), and a separately justified source decomposition defines \(h\). Zero support leaves \(Z\) undefined. Missing, contradictory and duplicated sources remain distinct states.
- **Formal claim/proof:** \(Z=u+(Q-u)/h\). For every pair \(c,d\), \(Q_c-Q_d=h(Z_c-Z_d)\); therefore argmax order and ties agree when \(h>0\). A target-policy change can change gradients, reliability and coverage, but the algebra alone changes no hard ranking.
- **Nonidentifiability from Q alone:** \(Q=(0.4,0.2,0.2,0.2)\) is compatible both with \(h=0.2,Z=(1,0,0,0)\) and \(h=1,Z=Q\). In general valid uniform-mixture support can range from \(1-K\min_c Q_c\) to 1 (apart from the \(h=0\) case). Thus entropy or the maximum of \(Q\) cannot uniquely recover source support.
- **Noise bound:** let estimated \(\hat Q,\hat h\) define \(\hat Z=u+(\hat Q-u)/\hat h\), with \(\hat h\ge h_{\min}>0\). Direct subtraction yields
  \[
  \|\hat Z-Z\|_\infty
  \le \frac{\|\hat Q-Q\|_\infty+|\hat h-h|\|Z-u\|_\infty}{\hat h}
  \le \frac{\epsilon_Q+(1-1/K)\epsilon_h}{h_{\min}}.
  \]
  The first numerator follows by writing \(\hat Z-Z=[(\hat Q-Q)+(h-\hat h)(Z-u)]/\hat h\). Approximation error bounds are LATENT unless established from arithmetic/provenance independently of semantic truth. No simplex projection is silently inserted.
- **Counterexample:** true support 0.02, estimated support 0.01, exact \(Q\), true \(Z=(0.8,0.1,0.05,0.05)\) produces an invalid inverse \((1.35,-0.05,-0.15,-0.15)\). Clipping/renormalizing would be an additional biased operator requiring its own analysis.
- **Closest prior/result:** elementary mixture deconvolution and data processing; no novel theorem claimed. This also collides with the existing repository's explicit Q/Z policy distinction.
- **Code/evidence:** ACTIVE_PIPELINE selects full historical Q; CURRENT states Q is not converted to Z. Toy probe verifies both decompositions and a tight numerical perturbation bound. Historical Q/Z pilot numbers are deliberately not imported.
- **Implication:** provenance-derived support and low-support rejection must precede any Z experiment. Q-to-Z alone cannot repair a systematically mislabeled RV source.
- **Falsifying control:** same \(Q\), different declared source support; \(h=0\); perturbed small \(h\); contradictory source labels; duplicate-source invariance. Check algebra and invalidity rather than reporting only downstream confidence.

## 5. Evidence card R1-P1 — noise-tolerant region naming with arbitrary source dependence

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT (elementary conditional lemma); method is PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED.
- **Plain statement:** an image-derived region can be named correctly despite some wrong anchors if enough total anchor weight points to its true dominant class. A student sufficiently close to the region target preserves that result. Independence is unnecessary.
- **Scope:** a fixed cine and finite partition; Hamming error, not Dice, clinical validity, optimizer convergence or new-domain guarantee.
- **Assumptions:** an image-derived partition contains disjoint nonempty regions \(C_j\), with normalized pixel measure \(\nu\). For each selected region there is a named class \(c_j\) satisfying
  \[
  \nu\{i\in C_j:Y_i\ne c_j\}\le\alpha_j\nu(C_j).
  \]
  Source outputs \(v_{js}\in\Delta^{K-1}\) have nonnegative weights \(a_{js}\) summing to one. There exists a good-source subset \(G_j\) with \(\sum_{s\notin G_j}a_{js}\le\beta_j\), and \(v_{js,c_j}\ge1-\eta_j\) for every \(s\in G_j\). No restrictions are placed on bad sources or on dependence. **\(c_j,\alpha_j,\beta_j,\eta_j\) are LATENT truth-dependent quantities.** A statement that these are known needs new evidence. Their existence does not follow from image smoothness or agreement.
- **Formal claim:** set \(t_j=\sum_sa_{js}v_{js}\), \(L_j=(1-\beta_j)(1-\eta_j)\). If each accepted pixel has student distribution \(p_i\) satisfying \(\|p_i-t_j\|_\infty\le\delta_j\) and
  \[
  2L_j-1>2\delta_j,
  \]
  then its unique argmax is \(c_j\). If all pixels in selected regions are accepted, their total error mass is at most \(\sum_j\alpha_j\nu(C_j)\), and conditional error is that quantity divided by selected coverage. If acceptance selects only some pixels, use impurity measured on that selected subset, or divide the whole-region error mass by actual accepted coverage; do not reuse \(\alpha_j\) as a conditional bound automatically.
- **Complete proof:** good-source mass gives \(t_{j,c_j}\ge(1-\beta_j)(1-\eta_j)=L_j\). For any \(d\ne c_j\), \(t_{j,d}\le1-t_{j,c_j}\le1-L_j\). Hence \(p_{i,c_j}-p_{i,d}\ge2L_j-1-2\delta_j>0\). All predicted labels on the region are \(c_j\); errors are confined to its impurity set. Add the disjoint error masses and divide by coverage when nonzero. At equality a tie is possible and no unique-label conclusion follows.
- **Noise-tolerant positive example:** four equally weighted sources put 0.9 on the true name and 0.1 on BG; a fifth source puts all mass on a wrong name. Then \(\beta=0.2,\eta=0.1,L=0.72\). Any per-pixel transfer error below 0.22 preserves the correct region name. With an exactly homogeneous region, the direct region estimator has zero error even though a pointwise noisy source pattern can be wrong on 20% of pixels. This is a possible improvement over a noisy teacher, not evidence it happens on patients.
- **Counterexamples / missing steps:** if all sources share a semantic swap, \(\beta\) or \(\eta\) violates the premise. A region crossing a cavity-wall boundary has large \(\alpha\). A feature graph can be smooth while connecting different anatomy. A source with no justified RV naming evidence cannot satisfy the RV premise just because it is the remaining bright component. Small average training CE does not imply the uniform transfer premise. A shared-encoder update can worsen it.
- **Closest prior:** robust weighted voting and label propagation; source aggregation in data programming. The proof is a convex-mixture margin argument, not a new statistical identification theorem. Allman-style independence assumptions are replaced here by a stronger *deterministic correctness-mass condition*, not removed without cost.
- **Mechanism collision:** claim → region naming and boundary recovery beyond coarse targets; nearest method → region pooling/graph label propagation plus fixed image partition; shared mechanism → propagate names within similar pixels; differing assumption → explicit dependence-tolerant contamination and region-purity budgets; unresolved gap → establish those budgets and a useful partition without target labels; simplest baseline → label the fixed regions directly, without training a student.
- **Code/evidence:** manufactured probe in this dossier only. CURRENT says the active detail head has no loss path, so this mechanism is not evidence for existing native-detail learning.
- **Implications:** test candidate-region quality and semantic assignment separately from transfer into a network. Native-resolution information enters through the image partition, not through resizing coarse Q. Test direct region voting before increasing model complexity.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** under manufactured controlled impurity and contamination, the strict inequality guarantees naming; violating it can yield confident wrong names. On permitted real evidence in a later approved phase, improvement must survive a direct-region baseline and per-class scrutiny. No claim can be certified from observed margin alone.

## 6. Evidence card R1-S1 — justified set-valued targets and the deterministic-ambiguity trap

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT; Cour et al. comparison is LITERATURE_RESULT. Set-target use remains PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED.
- **Plain statement:** a candidate set is supervision only if its membership assertion is justified; calling an unsupported guess a set does not make it safe.
- **Scope/assumptions:** a nonempty \(S_i\subseteq\mathcal O\) is a claim that \(Y_i\in S_i\), either logically under an explicit valid world family or with a declared probability/miscoverage model. The full set gives no semantic exclusion. The empty set denotes conflict, never BG.
- **Formal loss observation/proof:** \(\ell_S(p)=-\log\sum_{c\in S}p_c\) is minimized by any distribution supported on \(S\). Thus it cannot identify members within \(S\) without additional information or inductive assumptions. For \(S=\mathcal O\), the loss and gradient are identically zero. For \(S=\{\mathrm{RV},\mathrm{LV}\}\), swapping these two probabilities leaves the loss unchanged.
- **Membership counterexample:** an image can contain a bright vessel, atrium, artifact or non-target cavity that is BG under the four-class task. Blood-like appearance therefore does not establish \(Y\in\{\mathrm{RV},\mathrm{LV}\}\). This is a logical counterexample to an appearance-only rule; no patient or frequency claim is made. A valid exclusion needs the task ROI, anatomy/view relation and failure model.
- **Literature collision:** Cour, Sapp and Taskar assume the true label is in the observed candidate set. Their §3.1 ambiguity degree bounds conditional wrong-label inclusion; Proposition 1 gives \(R\le R_{\rm partial}/(1-\epsilon)\), while Proposition 3 handles a bad subset of mass \(\delta\). These are not permission to create candidate sets without true-label containment.
- **Important setting gap:** if \(S=g(X)\) is deterministic given the *full input available to the classifier*, any wrongly included \(c\ne Y\) has conditional inclusion probability either zero or one. At a point where it is included, the supremum ambiguity parameter is one. Merely varying deterministic heuristic candidate sets across images does not satisfy a uniform \(\epsilon<1\) assumption conditioned on exact \(X,Y\). Conditioning on a coarser feature variable changes the learner/information model; genuinely randomized candidates need an explicitly justified process. This blocks a facile import of that consistency result here.
- **Closest result / proof status:** partial-label learning already establishes useful learning under suitable stochastic ambiguity. The deterministic observation above follows by conditioning a measurable function on its argument. No novel general theorem claimed.
- **Implication:** keep anonymous region IDs, semantic exclusion constraints and full named targets separate. Sources may contribute only the information they justify. If only “not MYO” is justified, use that assertion; do not fill in a two-cavity ontology.
- **Limitations:** candidate-set training can be useful even when this particular sufficient theorem is vacuous. The failure of \(\epsilon<1\) is not an impossibility proof for every restricted hypothesis class or valid geometric prior.
- **Falsifying controls:** all-class set must yield zero semantic gradient; swap names inside an unresolved set; insert an out-of-ontology-looking region that maps to BG; create contradictory set intersections; check missing evidence never becomes an empty set or a forced label. Arithmetic probe verifies the deterministic inclusion probability equals one.

## 7. Evidence card R1-G1 — semantic generalization and abstention are additional claims

- **Status:** DERIVED_RESULT and COUNTEREXAMPLE; deployment proposals PROPOSED_NOT_APPROVED.
- **Plain statement:** selective confidence and successful TRAIN imitation cannot by themselves certify new-patient named correctness.
- **Scope/assumptions:** a fixed learner and an acceptance rule \(g(I)\); train and deployment observations obey the declared interface. Define coverage \(\kappa=P(g=1)\) and selective risk \(R_g=P(\hat Y\ne Y\mid g=1)\) only when \(\kappa>0\).
- **Formal statement/proof:** for any reference prediction \(t\), on the **actual** accepted population,
  \[
  R_g(\hat Y)\le R_g(t)+P(\hat Y\ne t\mid g=1).
  \]
  The error event is contained in the union of reference error and disagreement. Both population reference error and the deployment transfer term are unverified by finite TRAIN imitation alone. A useful bound must cover newly accepted pixels, including those outside teacher support.
- **Counterexample:** the two worlds of R1-I1 have identical predictions, margins, entropy, image reconstruction and consistency scores. Any common positive-coverage acceptance rule restricted to swapped pixels leaves the two selective risks summing to at least one. A confidence threshold cannot resolve their names. Zero coverage is abstention, not verified zero clinical risk; conditional risk is undefined there.
- **Generalization gap:** an unobserved target subpopulation can follow either of two semantic conventions or source-error patterns while all TRAIN evidence remains identical. A restricted, stable generative/anatomical model can exclude this possibility; the model is an assumption to test, not a consequence of self-supervision. Ben-David et al. gives stronger formal DA impossibility examples under its own assumptions.
- **Closest prior:** Geifman–El-Yaniv's selective-classification risk guarantee uses an i.i.d. labeled sample (Algorithm 1, Theorem 3.2). The no-task-mask setting here lacks that calibration ingredient. Their method is not a no-GT certificate.
- **Information boundary:** normalization on the current cine is compatible with a declared cine-input function. Fitting population moments/prototypes on held-out target cases, using manual view classification or consulting target teacher distributions is adaptation or added supervision and must be labeled separately. Ground-truth Hungarian alignment for scoring anonymous clusters does not supply a deployable fixed name mapping.
- **Evidence / implications:** fixed image-only or metadata-augmented inference must be declared before comparing methods. UNKNOWN must preserve the missing/conflict decision. Report coverage and dense/accepted behavior by named class; global coverage can hide total RV abstention.
- **Falsifiable controls:** vary the composition/order of a target cohort while holding the queried cine fixed. A fixed single-cine predictor must not change. Remove invalid metadata; remove all class-specific anchors; consistently relabel every source channel. Passing these is software/information evidence only, not an anatomical guarantee.
