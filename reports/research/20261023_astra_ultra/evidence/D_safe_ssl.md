## Strongest safe-reference collision: DS3L

The [supplementary proof, p. 2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/guo20i/guo20i-supp.pdf) makes zero unlabeled weights the feasible supervised fallback. Thus its theoretical comparison needs that fallback reachable in the admissible weighting family, plus the exact inner/outer argmin formulation. Alternating finite training is not certified merely by citing that result. This is conceptually close to Self-Audit's zero-displacement fallback, although Self-Audit's finite acceptance is measured directly on each current TRAIN audit slice.

The subaudit found a separate theory/implementation distinction in the official [WNet](https://github.com/guolz-ml/DS3L/blob/master/wideresnet.py#L1352-L1372) and [training code](https://github.com/guolz-ml/DS3L/blob/master/train.py#L730-L846): sigmoid weights cannot be exactly zero at finite parameters; Adam does not enforce the stated parameter unit ball, and the practical code has additional labeled consistency. Treat this as a source-level observation, not a reproduced failure or grounds to dismiss the paper.
