## Sources and discriminating limits

1. **OctreeNCA**, 2508.06993, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2508.06993?page=5 . Sections3.1–3.3, pp5–6: local recurrent state includes immutable input channels plus hidden channels. Learned convolution and two linear layers yield additive updates; random asynchronous firing. Octree levels each have separate NCA weights, hidden channels are upsampled to the next level. Global context from low resolution, native input at fine scale. Custom CUDA fuses updates to reduce allocations. Fig2 p4 is useful. Non-U-Net segmentation and shared local rules already exist. Parameters alone are not speed evidence; iterations, state memory, coarse/fine communication and custom kernels matter. Evaluations are prostate MRI, pathology and surgical video, not ACDC/M&Ms. Do not import reported memory advantages into our unimplemented student.

2. **NISF++**, 2608.00752, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2608.00752?page=5 . Sections3.1–3.3: a shared MLP takes physical coordinates, time and a learned128D patient latent, returns intensity and four-class segmentation fields. Learned per-slice pose/intensity corrections and PSF approximate imaging effects. Multiple SAX/LAX cine views; UKB120 patients,100/10/10 split. SAX labels from another segmentation model, manual LAX labels for part of TRAIN and TEST evaluation. New-subject inference fits latent/pose/scaling on images for2500 steps (~2minutes reported), weights frozen. This is an auto-decoder with per-subject optimization, not a one-pass image encoder or ACDC96 result. Primary text admits loss of fine detail; intensity fidelity need not improve segmentation. Root observed a possible time-encoding normalization ambiguity (sin(pi*t),cos(pi*t)); do not repeat as guaranteed cyclic without convention.

3. **CineMesh4D**,2605.13994, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2605.13994?page=3 . Sections2–3: multi-view cine encoders (2D and3D cardiac U-Net), fixed-connectivity mesh VAE, image-to-mesh mapping, global+local temporal module, differentiable contour renderer. Pretrained encoders and mesh prior; mesh references derived from manually refined segmentations and atlas deformation.222 subjects155/10/57, not ACDC/M&Ms. Direct original-image→mesh and render supervision already exist; changing output from pixels to mesh is insufficient novelty. Fixed topology and derived reference meshes constrain pathology claims. Methods even retain U-Net image encoders. Source dataset location is a placeholder 'xxx' in this manuscript, so provenance is incomplete.

4. **Codebook-Guided Cross-Modal Knowledge Distillation for Structurally Heterogeneous Features**,2609.37243, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2609.37243?page=4 . Sections3.3–3.5: teacher-side VQ codes, task/student compatibility, leave-one-code-out importance, selected shared teacher codes for student quantization, prediction matching. Explicitly handles grid-vs-sequence and has classification/NYU-depth segmentation experiments. This directly limits claims about shared concept codebooks, student-friendly selection, or merely replacing raw feature matching by discrete anchors. It does not establish a cardiac region-operator decoder, same-image semantic joint distributions, or a deployable action program. Need compare actual mechanism, not title.

5. **Relational Knowledge Distillation**,1904.05068, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/1904.05068?page=3 . Section3: pairwise distance and triplet-angle relational potentials, normalized distances, Huber losses. Transfers relations without matching feature coordinates. Section3.2.4 explicitly notes relational loss alone inadequate when individual output values/semantics matter; combine task/individual signal. Thus 'relations rather than features' and orthogonal/gauge invariance alone are old. A representation must drive decoding/computation and distinguish semantics, rather than merely adding RKD loss.

6. **Discovering Interpretable Algorithms by Decompiling Transformers to RASP**,2602.08857, https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2602.08857?page=4 . Section3: reparameterize GPT2-style Transformer into executable D-RASP, then causal pruning and primitive replacement. Exact theorem assumes exact linear layer-norm replacement. Expanded program may be exponential; simplification empirically aims to retain behavior. Controlled small algorithmic/formal-language tasks; not segmentation. Useful bridge for computation transfer, but no theorem that arbitrary cardiac teacher compiles to small fast program. Pruning uses KL and sparsity: computation transfer does not imply loss-free or lossless learning.
