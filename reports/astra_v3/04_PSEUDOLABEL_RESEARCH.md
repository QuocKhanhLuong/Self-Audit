# Pseudo-label research: evidence-bounded synthesis

**No verified source inspected here establishes scratch, mask-free, autonomously named RV/MYO/LV Dice .88–.90 on ACDC/M&Ms.** This is a bounded literature finding, not a universal impossibility claim or an estimated accuracy ceiling. The strongest route worth testing combines explicit semantic anchors, stronger image-only representation, verified temporal transport and bounded recruitment. It is a hypothesis with a failed local seed precursor, not a prediction of .90.

## What the scientific design must add

1. **An explicit named-anchor mechanism.** Bright blood pools alone do not distinguish RV/LV; a dark ring prior can mislabel extracardiac tissue and fail at base/apex. Image boundaries, trusted orientation, slice continuity and contraction correspondence are corroborating cues, not independent semantic votes by default.
2. **Image-only feature learning with a real task.** Masked reconstruction must hide the target across all neighbor channels. Feature consistency should compare spatially aligned anatomy; simple same-index frame consistency blurs moving myocardium. Current unmasked reconstruction is insufficient.
3. **Transport reliability.** Use forward/backward registration error, image mismatch, deformation Jacobian and cycle closure to abstain on unsupported correspondences. Through-plane motion and respiratory differences remain failure modes. Transport preserves class identity; it does not create it.
4. **Named prototypes rooted in accepted seeds.** Compute a class prototype from independently accepted pixels. Retain provenance and allow unknown classes; do not assign every anonymous cluster a mandatory name. Class balance heuristics must not manufacture absent anatomy.
5. **Bounded evolution.** Fix early learner steps, one recruitment round, and artifact freezes. Compare precision, coverage, named Dice, swaps, calibration and patient harm. Increased self-agreement or entropy collapse is not success.

Topology can rule out some configurations but cannot prove class identity for arbitrary anatomy. Fixed annulus assumptions are especially risky at basal/apical slices and in pathology. The local Round-0 already shows that intersecting stability cues can improve precision while destroying useful coverage. A topology arm identical to its anchor provides no empirical gain.

## Novelty stress test

Early learning/self-training, prototype updates, graph propagation, temporal consistency, abstention and image-only pretraining are established ingredients. Input-dependent sampling is established by [deformable attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00520); token-budget adaptation is established by [DynamicViT](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/747d3443e319a22747fbb873e8b2f9f2-Abstract.html). Combining them is not automatically a new method.

A defensible contribution would require showing that an explicitly specified semantic-conflict/transport criterion reduces named swaps and patient harm at matched coverage across ACDC and M&Ms, then yields a measurable deployment frontier. Current evidence establishes neither. Details and one chosen baseline are locked in [the experiment plan](09_EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md).
