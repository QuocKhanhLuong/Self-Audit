Self-Audit core, shared_benchmark, CUTS, DSS-US and SGSCN
([reports/ACTIVE_MILESTONE_SCOPE.md](../../reports/ACTIVE_MILESTONE_SCOPE.md)).

  history and provenance. Nothing here was deleted.

  v1) and has not been made or verified compatible with it. Its entrypoints do
  not enforce that environment.
- No STEGO run, result or readiness status counts toward the current milestone,
  and no official-run checklist requires it.
